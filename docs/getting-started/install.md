The **Busy Tag app** manages your device: designs, LED colours, focus sessions, integrations
and firmware updates. It is free and available on four platforms.

| Platform | Get it | Requires |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Windows | [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N48FS7QXQM5) or the direct installer from [busytag.com/software](https://www.busytag.com/software) | Windows 10 (1809) or later, x64 or Arm64 |
| macOS | [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/busy-tag/id6756409513?platform=mac) or the direct installer from [busytag.com/software](https://www.busytag.com/software) | macOS 12 or later |
| Android | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.greynut.busytag) | Android 7.0 or later |
| iOS / iPadOS | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/busy-tag/id6756409513) | iOS 15 or later |

## Store or direct installer?

On Windows and macOS you can choose between the store version and the direct installer from
busytag.com. Both have the same features. The differences:

- **Store versions** update themselves automatically through the store.
- **Direct installers** check for new versions themselves. Settings → **App info** shows the
  current and newest version and can download the new installer to your *Downloads* folder.
- **macOS:** the Mac App Store version is sandboxed, so firmware uploads over USB are slower. If a
  firmware update gives you trouble, install the direct version from
  [busytag.com/software](https://www.busytag.com/software).

## Languages

The app is available in English, Latvian, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Russian, Ukrainian,
Polish, Portuguese, Italian, Estonian and Lithuanian. Change the language under Settings →
**App Language**. Some translations are AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies.
