Highlights of recent Busy Tag app releases. Store versions can reach you a few days after the
release date. Your installed version is shown under Settings → **App info**.

## 1.7.6 — 30 September 2026

- Lower memory use in the Gallery and automatic clean-up of temporary files, image caches and
  logs.
- Sensitive parts of web addresses are removed from the app's logs.

## 1.7.5 — 29 September 2026

- **Manage access:** the owner of a shared device can see who has access and remove people.
- **Playlists and presets:** save integration playlists and focus-session presets.
- Integrations page: search, collapsible sections and **Rename** for every integration.
- **Status overlay** settings are available to everyone (firmware 3.0 or later).

## 1.7.4 — 11 September 2026

- New integration: **GitHub Copilot Usage**.
- Claude Code and Codex usage show remaining time in days, hours and minutes.
- Sign-in for integrations goes through a Busy Tag server, so no secrets are stored in plugins.
- Fixed outdated firmware information and update prompts after refreshing device info.

## 1.7.3 — 11 September 2026

- **Codex Usage** integration is available to everyone.
- Fixed a crash on iOS.

## 1.7.2 — 8 September 2026

- New integration: **Codex Usage**.
- Integration playback resumes after the app restarts.
- Android: update check and **Open on Google Play**.

## 1.7.1 — 13 August 2026

- New languages: Ukrainian, Estonian, Polish, Portuguese, Italian and Lithuanian.
- **Import design from link.**
- Windows and macOS: `busytag://` links open in the app.
- Fixes for device cloud sync and a Windows crash when selecting items.

## 1.7.0 — 6 August 2026

- Moved to .NET 10, with support for Android 16.

## 1.6.9 — 30 July 2026

- **Video to GIF:** turn a video clip (up to 20 seconds) into a GIF for a design.
- Russian language.
- New **⋯** menu for items in Designs, and accessibility improvements throughout the app.

## 1.6.5 – 1.6.8 — July 2026

- iOS and Android: sign-in for integrations that need an account (Google, Microsoft).
- Integration playlist countdown and better plugin configuration pages.
- Fixes for secure storage, popups and image resizing.

## 1.6.4 — 10 July 2026

- **Device sharing** improvements.
- Wi-Fi settings: enter a network name by hand, rescan networks, live signal strength.
- Windows: native Arm64 builds.
- Gmail and Google Drive integrations use narrower Google permissions.

## 1.6.1 – 1.6.2 — June 2026

- **macOS app** (Mac Catalyst).
- New Wi-Fi setup, device setup for the first device, and Wi-Fi signal indicator.
- Faster Gallery with offline caching and category browsing.
- Factory reset confirmation and automatic time zone for Wi-Fi devices.

## 1.6.0 — May 2026

- **Share devices** with other people using a QR code or link.
- **Share and import designs** as `.busytagdesign` files, links or QR codes.
- **Install integrations from a file** (`.busytagplugin`).
- New integrations: **Google Calendar**, **Gmail**, **Google Drive**, **Google Tasks** and
  **Claude Code Usage**.
- Redesigned integration settings pages.
