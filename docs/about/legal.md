## Your data

How the Busy Tag app, the Busy Tag cloud and the integrations handle your data is described in
the [Privacy Policy](https://busytag.com/privacy-policy/). In short: the app stores passwords and
sign-in tokens in the operating system's secure storage, and integrations that need an account
can be disconnected at any time in their settings.

## Open source

The [.NET library](https://github.com/busy-tag/busytag-lib) and the
[command-line tool](https://github.com/busy-tag/busytag-cli) are open source under the MIT
